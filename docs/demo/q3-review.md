## Why SMB is below plan in September

Two SMB renewals, Brightdesk and Calloway Foods, slipped from late September into October. Both were
signed on 3 October 2026 with no discount, so the revenue moves from Q3 into Q4: EUR 0.4 million.
Neither customer was lost. Logo churn stayed at 1.9%.
